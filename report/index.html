What your play really cost

Every hand you play is kept on this device, priced against perfect strategy, and split into three things: the casino’s built-in edge, your own decisions, and luck. Nothing is shown while you play, and nothing ever leaves your browser.

How to read this. For each hand, the page prices what the game’s house edge should cost on what you wagered, then what your decisions cost on top (the expected value you gave up against correct strategy), and treats everything left over as luck. Blackjack, Baccarat, Craps and Ultimate Texas Hold’em decisions are measured against basic strategy or the published edge of each bet; Pai Gow against the best possible set of your seven cards against the dealer’s face-up hand. Projections assume your average bet and your own swings continue, and are estimates, not predictions. This is a practice trainer for education only, not real-money gambling.
