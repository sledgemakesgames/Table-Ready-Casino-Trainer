Table manners

A few real-table habits worth knowing before you sit down at baccarat.

  • At mini-baccarat (the most common format) the dealer flips every card immediately — don't touch them. At bigger tables, the player with the largest bet may get to "squeeze" and slowly reveal their own cards, a real tradition, but check that it's allowed at your table first.
  • Once the dealer calls "no more bets," keep your hands off your chips until the hand resolves.
  • Chips go in the marked betting area — never handed straight to the dealer.
  • In commission games, the dealer tracks what you owe on the rail. You settle up when you leave or when asked, not after every hand.
  • Tipping the dealer (a "toke") is optional but common in North American casinos — place a chip near your bet and let them know it's for them.
