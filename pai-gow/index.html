Hand rankings

Standard poker hand rankings apply, with two Pai Gow-specific exceptions. The joker is semi-wild: it plays as an ace, or completes a straight, flush, or straight flush — whichever makes the best hand.

Five Aces (four aces + the joker) is the single highest hand, above even a Royal Flush.

The wheel (A-2-3-4-5) is a valid straight, but ranks as the second-highest straight — beaten by every other straight, even a low one like 6-5-4-3-2.

Rules cross-checked against Wizard of Odds, Pennsylvania Gaming Code §645a.6, and the official Hard Rock Atlantic City Face-Up Pai Gow Poker guide. The dealer's hand-setting logic here is a simplified house way, not the full casino-specific optimal ruleset.
